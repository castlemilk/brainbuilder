## Study shape

Plan for **12 people: four whose index episode primarily concerns interview preparation, four current-work technical fluency, and four general curiosity**. Ten or eleven completed interviews are acceptable only with at least three in each group and the imbalance explained. This is a small purposive sample for problem discovery, not a representative market survey.

Classify the primary job of a person's most recent eligible episode. Someone preparing for an interview while employed is not automatically a work-fluency participant. Secondary motivations can be noted, but one person contributes to only one primary cohort and to one overall participant count.

The main script does not assume short sessions, commuting, dissatisfaction, an interest in AI, willingness to pay, or a need for another app. It asks what happened, what already works and when learning is not the preferred use of time. A short Brainbuilder concept is optional and appears only after those questions.

## Important interpretation limits

The earlier planning material suggested roughly five independent people showing the same recurring job as a possible focus heuristic. A balanced first wave has at most four people in each primary cohort. **Do not count secondary jobs twice to manufacture five confirmations.** Make a provisional decision from this first wave; if the five-person confirmation rule is retained, propose a separate, authorized follow-up sample in the chosen cohort.

A participant's report of a past purchase is a self-report unless supporting material is voluntarily shown and narrowly assessed. Interest in a draft concept is not use, payment or evidence that the person learned. An observed action during an interview is not proof of their everyday behavior or later recall.
