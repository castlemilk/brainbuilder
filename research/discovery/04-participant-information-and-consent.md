## About the research

[Approved researcher/team and responsible study owner] is exploring how adults learn or practise things for technical interviews, current work or personal interest. We want to understand what people already do, what works for them and what they would keep or change.

You are being invited because you described a recent experience that fits this study. We plan to speak with around 10–12 people. This is not a test of your ability, an assessment by your employer or an offer to sell you something.

## What taking part involves

We will ask you to walk through one recent learning attempt, including what prompted it, what you used, what happened afterward and any other approach that worked better. We may ask about learning-related purchases or cancellations you choose to discuss. You may be shown a brief draft idea near the end, if there is time.

You do not have to buy or try a product. There is no promised personal learning benefit. You can skip a question, pause or end the conversation without giving a reason.

A camera, audio recording, showing material and future contact are all optional. Declining those options does not change whether you can take part or the approved thank-you terms. Participation does require agreement to minimal research notes; if you do not want notes taken, we will not begin the substantive interview.

Please describe work or interview situations at a general level. Do not share passwords, employer/client names, confidential code or documents, job applications, private messages, financial account details or another person's personal information. You can describe a material without showing it. If you choose to show something, you control what is visible and we do not keep a copy under this study's default plan.

## What we collect and who can access it

We propose to collect:

- Your separate contact/scheduling details, only to arrange this interview and handle any request you make.
- A study code, eligibility category, broad recruitment route and prior familiarity with Brainbuilder.
- Research notes about the experience, including brief words you say where useful, relevant choices and contextual details that avoid identifying you.
- A record of the information version you received and your participation, optional-audio and optional-recontact choices.
- Audio only if you separately agree and the approved recording setup is used.

[Named study owner and lead researcher] can access the contact register and the code-to-contact link. [Named moderator, note-taker and authorized analysts] can access only the session material they need. Other product contributors receive nonidentifying combined themes or paraphrases, not contact lists or raw recordings.

Names and contact details are kept separately from research notes. Coded notes can still be identifying if an unusual story is recognizable; they are restricted rather than described as guaranteed anonymous.

Raw interview material and contact details are not placed in the shared code repository, public pages or open spreadsheets. We will not publish an attributable quote, recording, screenshot or recognizable personal story without a separate specific decision and permission.

Under the proposed default, notes are manual, audio is off and automated transcription/AI processing of raw session material is off. If a different processing tool is proposed, its use, access and retention must be explained and agreed before it is used.

## Optional recording

You may choose audio recording to help the researcher check their notes. We do not need video recording, and your camera may remain off. The researcher will check your choice before switching on any recorder.

You may ask for recording to stop at any point. We will stop, check whether you want earlier audio retained or deleted, and agree whether to continue with notes. If a recording or transcription feature starts unexpectedly, the researcher will pause the session and address it with you.

Choosing notes only does not make your contribution less valuable.

## Proposed retention and withdrawal terms

These are the proposed terms to confirm before recruitment, not an already operating policy.

| Material | Proposed retention |
| --- | --- |
| Screening/contact information for people not selected or who decline before an interview | Delete within 14 days of the final selection decision or invitation closure |
| Optional audio and any separately approved raw transcript | Delete after notes are checked, and no later than 14 days after that interview |
| Contact details, scheduling information and the contact-to-study-code link for an interviewed participant | Keep through the 30-day withdrawal window; delete no later than day 37 after that interview after handling timely requests |
| Pseudonymous notes and consent/withdrawal administration records | Delete no later than 90 days after the approved wave-close date |
| Combined nonidentifying themes and decision summaries | Review at 12 months after wave close; remove source-level material and retain only nonidentifying product decisions that remain needed |
| Optional permission for one future-study contact | Expires 30 days after this interview; it does not enroll you in another study or allow marketing |

You can ask to remove the research material linked to you within **30 calendar days after your interview** by contacting [verified research contact] and giving your study code. You do not need to give a reason. The proposed handling target is acknowledgment within two working days and removal of active linked material within seven calendar days of the request.

Your code: [assigned after enrollment].

Your withdrawal-window end date: [calculated date].

If you lose your code, use the same private contact route used to arrange the interview; do not send an identity document. After the withdrawal window, we will explain what can still be located. The final storage plan must explain any trash, backup or service-retention limit before this document is approved. Do not promise that an active-file deletion instantly erases every service backup.

## Your choices

Record each choice separately. There are no prechecked optional boxes.

| Code | Statement | Choice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C01 | I have had the opportunity to read or hear the study information and ask questions. I understand that taking part is voluntary and I may skip questions or stop. | Yes / No |
| C02 | I agree to this interview and to the researcher taking the minimal pseudonymous notes described above. | Yes / No |
| C03 | I understand the stated access, use, retention and withdrawal arrangements for those notes and my contact/consent information. | Yes / No / Need clarification |
| O01 | I agree to optional audio recording for note checking under the stated terms. | Yes / No; default No |
| O02 | I agree to one contact within 30 days about a separate research opportunity. I understand this is optional and does not enroll me or permit marketing. | Yes / No; default No |

If C01 or C02 is No, or C03 is No/unclear, do not start the substantive interview. Answer questions or end without collecting learning-story details. O01 and O02 may be No while the interview proceeds with notes.

Consent can be documented by an explicit written response or by a spoken response logged manually before recording begins. Use only the approved method. Do not require a full legal name or public signature where the agreed pseudonymous method is sufficient.
