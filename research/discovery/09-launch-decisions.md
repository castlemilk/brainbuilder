## Before sending a screener

- [ ] L01–L10 are resolved; the final criteria and interpretation of L11 are documented.
- [ ] Exact invitation, screener and information versions have been approved for the named route.
- [ ] Participant-facing placeholders are gone and the thank-you terms match everywhere.
- [ ] Restricted destinations and access have been checked with blank/test data, not a real person's details.
- [ ] Recording/transcription/summary defaults have been observed and disabled unless explicitly part of the approved setup.
- [ ] Sampling/channel targets and the screening/selection rule are recorded before inspecting concept enthusiasm.
- [ ] Retention deadlines, withdrawal contact and actual wave-close date are consistent across all materials.
- [ ] External contact, publishing and any expenditure are explicitly authorized for this execution step.

## Before each substantive interview

- [ ] Eligibility, a concrete recent episode and the actual primary cohort are checked.
- [ ] The participant has the final information, knows who is present and can ask questions.
- [ ] C01–C03 are explicitly agreed; audio and future contact choices are separate and not preselected.
- [ ] Any format adjustment is supported without collecting a diagnosis.
- [ ] Contact details stay separate from session notes; the participant has a study code and withdrawal date.
- [ ] Optional audio is off unless both authorization and individual consent are present.
- [ ] The moderator will prioritize the real episode and alternatives before showing the concept.
