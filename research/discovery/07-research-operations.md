# Research operations

**PROPOSED OPERATIONS · BB-RES-003 · v0.1**

These are draft operating choices to finalize before collection. They are not a legal-compliance claim. No account, recording service, contact workflow or schedule is activated by this pack.

## 1. Roles and access

Name actual people before launch; no owner is assigned by this document.

| Role | Access needed | Responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Study owner | Approved project decisions and restricted administration | Authorize scope, resolve launch decisions and stop the study if necessary |
| Contact coordinator / lead researcher | Contact register and code-to-contact key | Screen, schedule, deduplicate known respondents, honor contact choices and handle withdrawal |
| Moderator / approved note-taker | Session code, consent status and necessary session material | Obtain choices, conduct the script, minimize identifying detail and check notes |
| Authorized analyst | Pseudonymous notes and minimal cohort/channel data | Interpret evidence and counterevidence; no contact list needed |
| Other product contributors | Approved nonidentifying combined findings | Use decisions without receiving raw participant records |

Use a named access list with the least access needed. Every observer must be disclosed to the participant. A role label is not proof that a person has been appointed or authorized.

## 2. Separate data locations

| Location | Contents | Default sharing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Approved private contact register | Preferred contact/alias, scheduling, contact choices, screening disposition and contact-to-code link | Contact coordinator and study owner only |
| Approved restricted consent/administration store | Information version, explicit choices, actual dates and deletion/withdrawal log | Named research personnel who need it |
| Approved restricted notes store | Pseudonymous episode records and analysis ledger | Named moderator, note-taker and analysts |
| Approved recording store, only if used | Consented audio and any separately approved transcript | Named moderator/note-checker; temporary |
| Shared repository/Space | Blank templates, synthetic rehearsal material and approved nonidentifying decision summaries | According to existing project access; no raw study records |

The tools and locations are unresolved launch decisions. Do not use public forms, open sheets, a personal meeting account with automatic summaries, or a new AI processing service by assumption. Confirm actual permissions, export/trash behavior and retention before choosing a tool.

Pseudonymous notes are still restricted: an unusual story can identify someone. Removing a name alone does not make a quote or employer-specific event safe to publish.

## 3. IDs, dispositions and denominators

Use a private screening code for each known respondent. Assign an enrolled participant code such as BB-DISC-Pnnn only once eligibility and participation are established. Never reuse a withdrawn person's code. Keep names, emails and referral identities in the contact register only.

Minimal private recruitment log fields: screening code, broad source/channel, known shared referral origin, eligibility decision/reason, primary cohort, prior familiarity, invited/scheduled/interviewed status, completion/withdrawal status, consent version and dates needed for deletion. Do not include a pain score or enthusiasm score as a selection field.

Keep counts distinct:

- Direct invitations count known unique people actually sent an authorized invitation. Public community reach remains unknown unless measured appropriately.
- Respondents, screened people, eligible people, people not selected, scheduled sessions, completed/partial interviews and withdrawals have separate counts.
- A person found through two channels is one participant; retain both routes where known and state the primary attribution rule.
- A no-response or decline is not evidence of low need. A screened-out person is not an interview completion.
- A replacement adds a new participant record without erasing the original dropout or withdrawal.
- Primary cohorts are mutually exclusive; secondary job tags do not add participants.

## 4. Before a session

Confirm the information and script versions, actual attendees, notes agreement method, optional choices, restricted storage permissions and the approved incentive/no-incentive wording.

Disable recording, automated transcription, meeting summaries and screen capture. Check the real tool settings rather than relying only on the meeting title. Audio is off unless both project authorization and participant consent are present. Do not record video.

Prepare a participant-specific withdrawal date and study code. Offer camera-off, supported typed format or breaks without asking for health details. Reschedule if the participant is driving or otherwise unable to attend safely.

## 5. Recording and artifact rules

Ask about recording separately from participation. Log the explicit choice manually before a recorder starts. An audio decline is not an eligibility failure.

If audio is used, record actual start/stop, tool and storage destination. If the person asks to stop, stop immediately, clarify whether earlier audio should be deleted, and agree whether to continue with notes. If the preference is unclear, do not retain optional audio while assuming permission.

Automated transcription or AI processing is off by default. Any proposal to use it needs a named tool, data-flow/access/retention review and clear participant information/choice before processing. Do not upload raw notes or audio to a service merely because an account exists.

Artifacts are optional and described by default. If shown, ask permission to take a nonidentifying paraphrase and let the participant control what is visible. Do not retain screenshots, private documents, receipts, proprietary code or account data under the default plan.

If an unapproved capture feature starts, pause the interview, stop the feature, identify the active copy, notify the participant and study owner, and agree on deletion and whether to continue. Record the operational issue without copying the captured content into the log.

## 6. Proposed retention schedule

All day counts below are calendar days unless stated otherwise. Final dates are computed from the approved collection window and actual session dates; the participant receives their own withdrawal date.

| Data | Proposed deadline |
| --- | --- |
| Screening/contact data for nonparticipants | Delete within 14 days of final screening disposition or invitation closure |
| Optional audio and any separately approved raw transcript | Delete when checked notes are adequate, no later than 14 days after the session |
| Contact register entries, scheduling and code-to-contact key for participants | Keep through day 30; delete by day 37 after the session after processing timely withdrawal requests |
| Optional future-study contact permission | Expires at day 30 after the session; no ongoing mailing list |
| Pseudonymous notes and consent/withdrawal administration | Delete no later than 90 days after the approved wave-close date |
| Combined findings and decisions | Review at 12 months after wave close; remove source-level material and keep only needed nonidentifying product decisions |

The approved wave-close date must be set before invitations. Do not extend it or retention silently. Any needed extension is a new documented decision with appropriate participant information.

Confirm how the chosen service handles trash, exports, local downloads and backups. Remove active copies and trash where supported. Record any residual backup policy accurately; do not promise immediate erasure of inaccessible backups or use that uncertainty as a reason to retain unnecessary active copies.

## 7. Withdrawal workflow

Proposed window: 30 days after the person's interview. Proposed service target: acknowledge within two working days and act on active linked material within seven calendar days.

1. Receive the request through the approved private contact route. Use the study code or original contact channel; do not ask for an identity document.
2. If identity is uncertain, confirm through the already recorded private route without disclosing research content to a new sender.
3. Pause further contact and processing for that person's linked material. Ask only what is necessary to distinguish ending contact, stopping recording or withdrawing the full research contribution.
4. Find notes, optional audio/transcript, exports, consent choices and any still-identifiable quotes/analysis entries. Remove the applicable active copies and derived participant-level references.
5. Preserve only a minimal disposition/action entry needed to account for the withdrawal and prevent unwanted follow-up. Do not repeat deleted story details in that entry.
6. Explain what was removed and what, if anything, is already irreversibly combined or subject to a disclosed backup retention limit.
7. Recheck scheduled messages, follow-up flags and deletion dates. Confirm completion through the approved route.

A request on day 21 should be actionable even though raw audio should already have been deleted by day 14. The code-to-contact link remains through the withdrawal window. Never treat “the recording was deleted” as proof that notes and quotes were removed too.

After day 30, explain honestly what can still be located and act where feasible; do not claim that a record is irreversibly anonymous while a usable identity link still exists.

## 8. Stop conditions and closure

Do not begin substantive questioning without the required participation/notes agreement. Pause for an undisclosed observer, unexpected recording, confidential material exposure, a power relationship or an unapproved tool. Resolve the issue or end the session; no target sample size overrides these conditions.

At wave close, reconcile achieved counts, remove duplicates, record missing/partial sessions, schedule only the approved manual deletion actions in the operations register, and produce the evidence-to-decision memo. This document does not create a calendar reminder or automation.

Only a separately approved export may enter the workbench. Synthetic fixtures, contact details, consent records, raw audio and source-identifying stories never enter its shared evidence exports.
