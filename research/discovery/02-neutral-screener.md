# Neutral screener

**UNSENT DRAFT · BB-RES-003 · v0.1**

This text becomes participant-facing only after the launch decisions and collection destination are approved. It is designed for a short written form or equivalent conversation. Do not use a public response sheet.

## Participant-facing introduction

We are planning interviews about how adults learn or practise things for interviews, current work or personal interest. We want to understand recent experiences, including things that went well, attempts that stopped and approaches you would keep using.

The interview is planned to take about 45 minutes. It is research, not a test of your ability or a sales call. Audio recording and showing any material would be optional. Completing these questions does not enroll you or require you to take part.

Who is conducting it: [approved researcher/team name and contact].
Thank-you arrangement: [approved amount and terms, or explicit statement that there is no payment].
How screening responses are handled: [approved restricted collection link and brief privacy/retention wording from file 04].

Please avoid names of employers or clients, private documents and other people's personal information.

## Questions in this order

**S01. Are you 18 or older?**

- Yes.
- No.
- Prefer not to answer.

A “No” or “Prefer not to answer” ends screening without collecting further background. Do not ask for a date of birth.

**S02. Would you be able to take part in an English interview, spoken or typed, of around 45 minutes with breaks if needed?**

- Yes.
- Yes, with a format adjustment.
- No.
- Unsure.

If an adjustment would help, optionally describe the format you prefer, such as camera off, typed answers or breaks. Please do not provide a diagnosis or other health information. The researcher will confirm feasibility before scheduling.

**S03. In the last eight weeks, was there a particular occasion when you tried to understand or practise something? An unfinished or abandoned attempt counts.**

- Yes.
- No.
- Unsure.

If yes or unsure: in one or two sentences, what was it, roughly when was it, and what prompted you? Approximate timing is enough. If no specific recent example can be identified, do not move into questions about a proposed product.

**S04. What was the main reason for that particular attempt?**

Choose the closest description, then explain in your own words.

- Preparing for a technical hiring interview.
- Understanding something technical for a current work task or discussion.
- Exploring something mainly because it interested me personally.
- A required course, certification or other examination.
- Another reason.
- More than one reason / not sure.

If more than one reason: which reason most influenced your decision to do it at that time? You may still describe the other reasons. Do not name the employer, interviewer or client.

**S05. What did you first do or use on that occasion, and where did you leave it?**

A book, conversation, existing knowledge, course, app, search, notes, practice or doing nothing further can all be relevant. There is no preferred answer. Do not ask the person to rate how frustrating it was.

**S06. Before this invitation, had you heard of or used Brainbuilder?**

- No.
- Heard of it, not used it.
- Seen a concept/demo.
- Used it.
- Unsure.

If yes, a brief description is enough. Record familiarity for interpretation; do not automatically reject a positive or negative user.

**S07. Where did you come across this invitation?**

Name the channel or route if known. Do not provide another person's private contact information. “I do not know” is a valid answer.

**S08. Do you have a work, assessment or service relationship with the researcher or the Brainbuilder team that could make it uncomfortable to decline?**

- No.
- Yes.
- Unsure.
- Prefer not to answer.

No names or explanation are required. The researcher must resolve a potential power relationship before proceeding.

**S09. Would you like the researcher to contact you about this interview if there is a suitable place?**

- Yes.
- No.

If yes, use the separate approved private scheduling route: [contact form or researcher address]. Ask only for a preferred contact method/address and a name or alias to use. Availability/time zone can be requested later for scheduling. Do not put contact information into the analysis answers.

This is permission to arrange this interview, not permission for future marketing or another study.

## Researcher-only routing

| Condition | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| S01 is not “Yes” | Ineligible for this adult-only wave; stop before substantive collection |
| No concrete eligible episode within eight weeks after one neutral clarification | Ineligible for this wave; retain only the minimal reason code |
| Main episode clearly matches INT, WORK or CUR | Assign that one primary cohort; record secondary motivations separately |
| Certification/exam-only or another purpose outside the three jobs | Outside this wave; do not relabel as WORK merely because the person is employed |
| Multiple/unclear purposes | Pending clarification using the question in file 01; do not choose a cohort to fill a slot |
| Potential power relationship or team membership | Exclude or pause for conflict review; no pressure to explain |
| Declines audio, camera, artifact sharing, payment discussion or future contact | Remains eligible if participation and minimal note-taking are agreed |
| Unfamiliar with or skeptical of Brainbuilder, satisfied with alternatives, or uses long sessions | Remains eligible; preserve the contrary case |
| Format adjustment requested | Check whether it can be supported; do not collect a diagnosis |
| Eligible but a cohort/route target is filled | Eligible / not selected for this wave; do not code as “no need” or “declined” |

Selection is purposive and must not favor enthusiastic or dissatisfied respondents. Use the predetermined cohort and channel targets. No product pitch, price anchor, AI preference question, disposable-income question or diagnosis belongs in this screener.

## Minimal screening record

Use a private pseudonymous screening ID and store only: approximate episode timing, brief nonidentifying topic, primary/secondary job, recent attempt present/uncertain, broad channel/referral cluster, prior product familiarity, eligibility disposition and reason, format accommodation, recruitment contact choice, and screening version.

Keep contact details and any name/alias in the separate contact register. Do not require a CV, LinkedIn profile, job application, proof of purchase, screenshot, exact employer, address, salary, birth date or account identifier.
